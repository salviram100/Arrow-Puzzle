Privacy Policy

Application: Arrow Puzzle  |  Effective Date: August 29, 2026

Summary: Arrow Puzzle is built by Velocity Games as an offline-first puzzle game. We value your privacy: we do not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information to external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how Velocity Games ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information in connection with our mobile application, Arrow Puzzle (the "Application").

By downloading, installing, or playing Arrow Puzzle, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these policies, please do not use the Application.

2. Information We Collect

Arrow Puzzle is an offline-first game. We do not require account registration with email or passwords, and we do not collect personal identifiers.

  • Local Profile & Gameplay Data: When playing as a guest, your chosen player nickname (GamerTag), avatar selection, stage progress (1–100), star ratings, high scores, and custom workshop levels are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore.
  • User Preferences: Game audio toggles (sound effects), haptic vibration preferences, and visual glow settings are saved exclusively on your local device.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, contacts, or financial details.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

The Application may request or utilize standard device capabilities to enhance gameplay:

  • Vibrate / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile feedback during move collisions or stage clearances. This can be toggled off at any time in Game Settings.
  • Local Storage Sandbox: Used exclusively to save and load your stage completions and custom created levels within the protected app sandbox.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties. The Application is distributed via the Google Play Store and adheres strictly to Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Standard platform services provided by Google (such as the Google Play runtime environment and Android OS) may process anonymous system telemetry according to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all game progress and custom stages reside solely on your physical device:

  • You can erase all campaign progress at any time directly within the app by going to Settings > Reset All Stage Progress.
  • Uninstalling the Application or clearing the app data via Android system settings will permanently remove all locally stored progress and preferences.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Puzzle is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Because all data is retained within the Android operating system's application sandbox, your game state benefits from Android's built-in sandbox security and encryption mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates to the Application or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
